From blank page to final cut in five moves.

  1. 01

    Script

    We interview your team, find the one idea worth saying and write it tight — usually inside three days.

  2. 02

    Storyboard

    Frame-by-frame boards and style frames, so you see the film before a single second is animated.

  3. 03

    Voiceover

    Cast from our roster of narrators or use your own. Recorded, timed and approved before animation starts.

  4. 04

    Animation

    Design, motion and sound come together. You review a rough cut, then a polished cut — two revision rounds included.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Master files in every format you need: web, social cut-downs, captions and a clean project archive.
